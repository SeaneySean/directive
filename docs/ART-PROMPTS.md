# Art prompts for GPT Image 2.5

Generate each, save under `public/assets/art/` with the exact filename, and the art slice
picks them up. Keep the same style seed as the splash: photographic-painterly, dark, gold and
teal light, film grain. Everything must be **free of text** unless stated.

## 1. World map backdrop — `world-map.png` (1920x1080)

> A flat map of the whole Earth seen from above at night, equirectangular projection, all
> continents visible and correctly placed, city lights glowing warm gold, oceans near-black
> teal, faint golden network lines connecting continents, small glowing gold triangle markers
> scattered, subtle film grain, cinematic conspiracy thriller mood. No text, no labels, no
> borders drawn, no UI. Dark enough that white text placed on top is readable.

## 2. Event card illustrations (each 1024x768, no text)

- `event-candidate.png`: A grinning reality-TV star on a stage in a sharp suit under gold
  confetti, hooded figures in the shadows of the wings, TV cameras, gold and black.
- `event-leak.png`: A Nevada desert road at night, an unmarked hangar with a single light,
  chain-link fence and a "restricted" feel, a faint disc-shaped silhouette in the sky.
- `event-whistleblower.png`: A nervous man in a raincoat handing a folder to a journalist in
  a car park at night, rain, one sodium street lamp.
- `event-miracle.png`: A crowd in a sunlit square looking up in wonder, water rising from a
  well in a golden column, hopeful and warm.
- `event-summit.png`: Two hooded delegations meeting across a long black table in a
  candlelit stone hall, a gold eye-in-triangle on the far wall.

## 3. Mission briefing backdrops (each 1920x1080, no text)

- `briefing-area-51.png`: Interior of a vast military hangar at night, crates, a covered
  craft under tarpaulin, security lights, guards' silhouettes.
- `briefing-atlantis.png`: Flooded ancient ruins under a cavern ceiling, bioluminescent teal
  water, broken columns, a glowing orichalcum tablet on an altar.

## 4. Agent portraits (each 512x512, no text) — `portraits/cole.png`, `diaz.png`, `okafor.png`, `reyes.png`

> Head-and-shoulders portrait of a special operative in dark tactical gear, neutral
> expression, lit from one side in gold, black background, consistent style across all four.
> Cole: woman, 30s, cropped dark hair. Diaz: man, 40s, grey stubble. Okafor: man, 30s, shaved
> head. Reyes: woman, 20s, long hair tied back.

## 5. Unit sprites (optional; only if Kenney's miniatures don't fit) — `units/*.png`

> Isometric 3/4-view game sprite of a soldier in dark tactical gear holding a rifle, standing,
> facing down-right, on a fully transparent background, clean edges, 256x256, painterly but
> readable at 64px. Variants: `soldier.png`, `guard.png` (grey camo, cap), `guardian.png`
> (bronze armoured figure with a trident), `sectoid.png` (small grey alien).
